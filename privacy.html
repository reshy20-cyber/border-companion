English

  • We do not collect any personal data. The app has no sign-in, no accounts, no ads, and no tracking or analytics.
  • What you enter (nationality, purpose of travel, trips, dates, stay counter) is stored only on your device in the browser's storage and is never sent to any server. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser or by uninstalling the app.
  • The "Save a backup" button creates a file on your own device; we never see its contents.
  • The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services may log standard technical data such as your IP address under their own policies.
  • Buttons that open external sites (official government sites, a Google search for your embassy) take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies. The app does not pass any of your data to them.
  • The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from children.
  • The legal information in the app is guidance only, not legal advice.
  • Contact: feedback page on GitHub.
