## English (en)

**App name (max 30):**
Border Companion: Travel Law

**Short description (max 80):**
Visas, entry rules, residence and local laws in 28 countries, by nationality.

**Full description:**
Border Companion is your legal guide before you travel and while you stay.

Pick your passport, purpose of travel and destination, and the app tells you right away whether you need a visa, can enter visa-free, need an e-authorisation or can get a visa on arrival, with a button that opens the official application site, not a middleman.

✈️ What's inside:
• Entry status for your nationality in 28 countries across Europe, the Gulf, the Arab world, Türkiye, the US, Canada and Asia.
• Residence & immigration: the responsible authority, mandatory registrations and overstay penalties.
• Currency and cash declaration limits.
• Driving: speed limits, tolls, international permits.
• Laws and customs that can get visitors into trouble.
• Watch out: common tourist scams and fake websites in each country.
• Emergency numbers and a button to find your embassy.
• What do I do if…? Lost passport, stay ending, stopped by police, car accident.

🧳 Trip tools:
• My trips: a ready document checklist per trip, with an automatic passport-validity check.
• Stay counter, including the Schengen 90/180-day rule.
• My dates: alerts before your passport, visa or residence expires, plus calendar reminders.
• Backup and restore.

🔒 Privacy: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your device.
🌐 Four languages: Arabic, English, French, German.
📶 Works offline after the first open.
🔄 Information reviewed monthly.

Note: guidance only, not legal advice. Always confirm with the official authority or embassy before you travel.
